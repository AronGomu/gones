Partial-cache reads and the backfill race

You found a real gap. A Season spans years, Tournaments are partitioned by year, so expanding a Season can demand a partition that has not landed yet — and a second fetch path writing the same partition as the background backfill is a genuine race. Five questions and the spec is closed.

Archive rebuild → Model ✓ Table ✓ Ratings ✓ Locking ✓ Naming ✓ → Partial reads Write ownership

Your three questions, restated as the gap they expose

1 · Is there a specific request to fetch a missing Tournament? The spec never said. Today it only has a year-partition fetch, so an expanded Season whose Tournaments live in an unloaded year has no defined behaviour at all.

2 · Does that request write to IndexedDB, and does it conflict with the backfill? This is the sharp one. If an on-demand fetch writes the same year partition the background backfill is also writing, there are two writers with no ordering — and worse, it breaks the invariant the whole cache rests on: that a year partition is atomically present or absent. A partially-filled 2023 that the client believes is complete would silently hide Tournaments forever.

3 · If the Tournament is already local, is the API still called? Partly settled, never stated: a locked Tournament is never refetched, and an unlocked one follows the ADR 0039 TTL — cached under 24h, refetched beyond. Q5 states it explicitly.

Measured before proposing — Seasons do not need partitioning

Half of this problem disappears if the Seasons catalog is fetched whole rather than by year, the way the Leagues catalog already is. A Season row measures 270 chars / 540 B UTF-16, 300 / 600 B with a tournamentYears field added:

All comfortable in IndexedDB, which has no 5 MiB cap. Seasons are 10–30× fewer than Tournaments by construction, so partitioning them buys little and costs the "which year is this Season in?" question — a Season spanning Dec 2025 to Mar 2026 has no single answer. Q1 proposes dropping it.

1. Is the Seasons catalog year-partitioned?

Round 4 froze /api/archive/league-seasons/all?year=YYYY, but a Season spanning December to March has no single year, and the measurements above say partitioning buys almost nothing here.

2. Opening a Season whose Tournaments are not loaded yet

Your central question. Three fundamentally different answers, and they differ most in whether a second write path exists at all.

3. How does the client know which years a Season needs?

Whatever Q2 chooses, the client must know which partitions to demand before it can demand them. A Season currently carries lastTournamentDate and tournamentCount — enough to know rows are missing, not enough to know where they are.

4. What owns writes to a year partition?

The invariant your question threatens. Whatever Q2 chooses, this states plainly who may write a partition and what "this year is cached" means — so a partially-filled year can never be mistaken for a complete one.

5. If the Tournament is already local, is the API called?

Your third question. The pieces are settled — locked rows are never refetched, ADR 0039 gives unlocked data a 24-hour TTL — but the combination has never been written down as one rule.

Facts resolved for this round — do not re-answer

  • player_statistics is keyed on PlayerName alone (PlayerStatisticsReadModelConfigurations.cs:13). Per-scope rows need a composite key — (scope_kind, scope_id, player_name).
  • Row multiplication is bounded and mild: a player who competed in 3 Seasons across 2 Leagues holds 1 global + 3 season + 2 league = 6 rows. Rebuild cost is ~3× today's, because each Tournament is replayed into exactly three scopes.
  • The rebuild is already transactional and whole-archive (PlayerStatisticsRebuildService.RebuildAsync), so adding scopes changes its size, not its shape or its guarantees.
  • catalog-cache.ts is localStorage-only by construction and its header says so; ADR 0039 states "public data caches in localStorage". Moving the archive catalog to IndexedDB amends that clause.
  • server-authority-boundary.test.ts:107-111 allowlists indexedDB to exactly three files: indexed-db.ts, local-league-archive-backend.service.ts, local-live-backend.service.ts. A new public catalog store adds a fourth and the test must be updated deliberately, not incidentally.
  • Measured row sizes, real schema: Tournament catalog row 282 chars / 564 B UTF-16; Season row 270 chars / 540 B.

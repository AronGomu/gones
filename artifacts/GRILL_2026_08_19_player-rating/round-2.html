Tune the Glicko-2 engine and its surfaces

Round 1 settled what the rating is. Eleven decisions were unblocked by those answers: the numbers Glicko-2 needs, the edge cases the archive actually contains, and where the rating shows up. One question resolves a contradiction between two of your own Round 1 answers.

Settled in Round 1 — not re-asked

  • Glicko-2, one rating period per tournament, match-result driven with a margin-of-victory multiplier.
  • Global rating only. Completed tournaments only. Server data only — local archives never contribute.
  • Byes ignored; draws score 0.5. Fewer than 5 tournaments = provisional: shown, flagged, sorted to the bottom by tournaments played then matches played.
  • Stored by extending the ADR 0040 read model with a full deterministic replay; formula version bumped.
  • Default sort of the global rankings. Display = integer plus change since last tournament.
  • Inactivity: frozen rating + decaying confidence + inactive players off the default leaderboard, and a true-decay number computed alongside, off by default.

New facts scouted for this round

  • validateMatch caps each score at 0–2 and rejects nothing else: 0–0, 1–1 and 2–2 are all valid matches today, and 0–0 is byte-identical to an unscored match. Margins are therefore only 2–0, 2–1, 1–0.
  • Nothing anywhere counts tournaments played per player — the 5-tournament gate needs a new counted field, not just a filter.
  • player_statistics has one indexed column per statistic (8 indexes today) and GLOBAL_STATS_SORTABLE_COLS mirrors them one-for-one; sorting and paging happen in the browser over the TTL-cached catalog.
  • Exports carry league documents only — PUBLIC_EXPORT_V4_LEAGUE_FIELDS = ['id','name','status','tournaments']. No statistic is exported; everything is re-derived on restore.
  • Config precedent for an optional behaviour already exists: Gones:PlayerStatistics:RebuildOnStartup, a const key with a default.
  • The player page's Online only toggle is a real, persisted preference (player-online-only-toggle) that merges browser-local leagues into the displayed statistics.

Contradiction to resolve — Q9 below. Your Q4 answer was "leagues keep Swiss standings, the rating stays global-only"; your Q10 answer was "league standings keep Swiss points, with rating as an extra column". One says the rating never appears in a league table, the other says it does.

1. Which Glicko-2 seed constants and system constant?

Glicko-2 needs a starting rating, a starting deviation and a τ that caps how violently volatility reacts to surprises; these numbers define how fast a rating moves and how far apart the top and bottom end up.

2. How are rating periods ordered when two tournaments share the same date?

Round 1 removed order inside a tournament, but periods are still replayed in sequence, and tournamentDate has no clock — two same-day tournaments have no defined order today.

3. What shape does the margin-of-victory multiplier take?

Scores are capped at 2, so the only real margins are 2–0, 2–1 and 1–0 — the multiplier has three inputs, not a continuum, and it must not break Glicko-2's expectation maths.

4. How are equal scores and 0–0 matches treated?

Validation accepts 0–0, 1–1 and 2–2 as valid matches, and a 0–0 is indistinguishable from a match nobody scored — feeding those to a rating would quietly invent draws that never happened.

5. What counts as inactive, and what does "dropping off the default leaderboard" mean?

Round 1 chose to push inactive players off the default board; that needs a cutoff and a behaviour, or the rankings silently lose people with no explanation.

6. How is the true-decay rating computed and exposed so it can be switched on later?

You asked for both numbers — frozen and decaying — with decay computed but not shown by default, which is a storage and a toggle decision, not a maths one.

7. Does the read model gain rating columns only, or a rating history table?

Showing ±N since the last tournament needs at least the previous rating; a chart later needs every period — and a history table multiplies what the in-transaction rebuild has to write.

8. Do matches against provisional players move a ranked player's rating?

With a 5-tournament gate, a large share of every event's matches are against unranked players — excluding them would starve the ranked pool, including them naively imports their noise.

9. Does a league standings table show the rating at all? (resolves the Q4 / Q10 contradiction)

Q4 said the rating stays global-only; Q10 said league standings gain a rating column. Both cannot ship, and the difference is visible on every league page.

10. What does the player page show when Online only is off?

With the toggle off, the page's statistics include browser-local leagues, but the rating is server-computed by decision — so the rating and the statistics beside it describe different match sets.

11. What is the ±N delta measured against, and where does it appear?

"Change since the last tournament" needs one fixed baseline, or the number differs between the rankings row and the player page.

1. Which tables are in the rename set?

"Rename everything" is unimplementable literally — some Tournament-named tables belong to other domains, and one is a shared lookup.

2. Do the old /api/tournaments/* paths keep working?

Installed PWAs and the offline read cache hold URLs; but ADR 0022 set a precedent of no API aliases.

3. Canonical public route for an event page?

/events/:slug is currently a redirect into /calendar/tournaments/:slug; after the rename the arrow must flip, and the calendar prefix may or may not survive.

4. Removing an org's last organizer — what happens?

You said an organization cannot exist without at least one organizer; the system must do something concrete at that boundary.

5. Creation side of the same invariant?

The mirror of Q4: if orgs must always have a member, the create action cannot leave one empty.

6. Existing violating rows — what does the migration do?

Today's DB may hold member-less orgs and Organizers with no membership; the invariant needs a defined starting point.

7. User picker at hundreds of users — confirm the shape?

You flagged the scale; loading all users into a client-side autocomplete stops working around a few hundred rows.
